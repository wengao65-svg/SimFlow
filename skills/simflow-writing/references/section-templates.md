# Section Writing Index

Use this index to select section guidance, not as a fill-in manuscript template.
Sentence counts, paragraph counts, section order, and figure counts follow the
argument and supplied publication requirements, not the reference corpus.

## Section Choices

- `abstracts.md`: select the question, finding, informative evidence, and bounded
  implication without a fixed sentence skeleton.
- `introductions.md`: establish what is known and what this work addresses;
  do not default to a simulation-cost argument.
- `results.md`: develop observations, comparisons, and explanations through
  scientific dependencies rather than completed-work chronology.
- `discussions.md`: synthesize implications and limits without mandatory moves.
- `methods.md`: specify what was done and how quantities were obtained.
- `figure-captions.md`: supply the definitions needed to interpret each figure.
- `narrative-examples.md`: distinguish audit summaries from academic narrative
  and inspect evidence-preserving revision cases.

The same paper may combine Results and Discussion or put theory before results.
Do not force a dedicated mechanism section, validation figure, prediction panel,
or future-work conclusion when the contribution does not support it.

## Contribution Planning

Privately summarize what the work establishes and why the evidence supports it.
Use the summary to test coherence, not as a mandatory "We introduce X, enabling
Y by overcoming Z" sentence. A physical result need not resolve a mechanism;
a theoretical result need not enable larger MD; a database need not establish a
universal law. Preserve the actual contribution and its limits.

## Reviewer Responses

For requested response letters, read `reviewer-checklist.md`. Address the concern,
describe the actual analysis or clarification, cite its manuscript location,
and explain whether it changes the conclusion. Do not claim a test was run
when it was only proposed. Point-by-point structure belongs to this deliverable,
not automatically to the manuscript being revised.
